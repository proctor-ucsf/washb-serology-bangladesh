## Study overview

Using data from the WASH Benefits Bangladesh cluster-randomised trial, the study evaluated the effects of water, sanitation, and handwashing (WASH) and nutrition interventions on enteric pathogen serological outcomes among children in rural Bangladesh. The analyses examined pathogen-specific IgG responses to 12 enteric pathogens during the first two years of life.

## Repository contents

This repository contains statistical analysis scripts and supporting materials used to generate the analyses, tables, and figures reported in the manuscript. These materials are provided to support reproducibility of the study findings.
